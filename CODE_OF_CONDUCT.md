# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

UniTask is a project maintained independently, and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome.

As contributors and maintainers, we are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, experience, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal background, race, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that help create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate and constructive.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully.
- Disagreeing with ideas without attacking the people behind them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting different levels of experience and helping newcomers where practical.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-related communication. It also applies when someone is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub: [@ahmad-maqsood](https://github.com/ahmad-maqsood).

Please include a description of what happened and any relevant links or context. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports, respect the privacy of those involved as far as practical, and decide on an appropriate response. Depending on the circumstances, this may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces.

The maintainer may also take action in response to behaviour that is harmful to the community even if it is not explicitly listed above.

## Attribution
