UniTask Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: your courses, tasks and notes stay on your phone unless you choose to sign in or back up. UniTask does not sell your data and does not show ads. The Google Drive backup goes into a hidden folder in your own Drive that only UniTask can open.

UniTask ("the app") is an Android app for students to manage courses, assignments, deadlines, a class timetable and notes. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends to other services, and the choices you have.

1. Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following on your device, in its private app storage:

  • Courses, timetable entries, assignments, your own tasks and notes (including note pictures).
  • Files you attach to assignments.
  • App settings such as theme, notification sound and reminder options.

This data is not sent anywhere unless you use one of the optional features below. Uninstalling the app removes it from your phone.

2. Sign in with Google (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to use study groups, group tasks and chat. When you sign in, the app receives your Google account email address and basic profile information from Google, and uses it to identify your account in the app.

If you use these features, the data you add to them (for example group tasks and chat messages) is stored in a cloud database provided by Supabase. Other members of a group can see what you share in that group. Your own courses, tasks, timetable and notes are not uploaded to Supabase. Sign-in is handled through Google.

If you never sign in, none of this applies.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

You can back up the app to your own Google Drive, and restore it on a new phone. To do this, the app asks for one Google permission: "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (drive.appdata).

  • The backup is stored in a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive. Only UniTask can read or write this folder.
  • UniTask cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files.
  • The backup contains your courses, timetable, assignments and their attachments, your own tasks, notes with pictures, and app settings.
  • The backup goes from your phone directly to your Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer, and the developer cannot read it.
  • The backup counts toward your Google Drive storage.

UniTask's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you asked for, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone.

4. Checking for updates

The app checks GitHub for a newer release automatically when you open it (at most once every 10 minutes), and when you tap the update button. This is a normal web request to GitHub, which may see your IP address as any website would. No personal data from the app is included.

About every 15 minutes, even when the app is closed, the app also contacts Supabase to check for new announcements and, if you are signed in, for new group tasks and chat messages so it can show notifications.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to remind you about deadlines and classes on your phone.
  • Internet: for sign-in, groups, announcements, Google Drive backup and update checks.
  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a study group invite QR code. The camera image is read on your phone to find the code. It is not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Files you pick: when you attach a file or open a file with the app, only the file you choose is read.

6. Sharing

UniTask does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not share your data with third parties for advertising. Your data is handled only by the services needed for the features you use: Google (sign-in and Google Drive) and Supabase (groups, chat and announcements).

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Phone data: delete items inside the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Google Drive backup: in the app, choose Disconnect. To delete the backup itself, open Google Drive, then Settings, Manage apps, UniTask, and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove UniTask's access in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.
  • Cloud data (groups and chat): in the app, open Settings, then Online account, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your online account. Groups you own are handed to another member, or deleted if you are the only member. Messages, group tasks and group courses you created are removed for everyone in those groups. Your courses, tasks and notes on this phone are not touched. You can also email the address below and ask for your data to be deleted. We will delete it.

8. Children

UniTask is meant for students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
